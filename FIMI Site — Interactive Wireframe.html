The evidence

Diet-related disease is fueling a health and economic crisis

85% of all health care spending is related to managing diet-related chronic conditions
Suboptimal diets and food insecurity cost the U.S. economy $1.1 trillion annually in health care spending and lost productivity

Our evidence shows:

Nutrition interventions reduce hospitalizations, improve outcomes, and lower costs.

$13.6B
could be saved annually if all eligible patients received medically tailored meals
49%
of hospitalizations could be averted for adults receiving 10 medically tailored meals/week over 9 months
292,000
cardiovascular events could be prevented through produce prescriptions

How we advance Food is Medicine policy

Preparing policymakers and regulators for every kind of engagement

Congressional Briefings

Staffing and content support for member and staff briefings on Food is Medicine research and policy.

Testimony

Drafting written statements and preparing spokespeople for congressional testimony.

Bill Language

Technical assistance translating Food is Medicine evidence into legislative text.

Remarks & Statements

Talking points, floor statements, and other remarks grounded in the latest research.

Public Comments

Written comments submitted directly to federal agencies in response to proposed regulations and requests for information.
